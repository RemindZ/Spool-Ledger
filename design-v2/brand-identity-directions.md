# Spool Ledger identity system

**Primary product name:** Spool Ledger  
**Permanent descriptor:** Bambu Filament Migrator

Spool Ledger is the selected identity for the independent community utility. The descriptor remains visible in product chrome, repository artwork, installer surfaces, documentation, and release material so the application remains easy to find and understand.

## Identity idea

Two open spool arcs surround a neutral profile-data tile. A short orange filament tail exits the tile and ends at a green registration notch. The system connects physical filament, inspectable profile data, and a controlled destination without resembling recycling, cloud sync, or the marks of Bambu Lab or OrcaSlicer.

## Name hierarchy

1. **Spool Ledger** is always the dominant name.
2. **Bambu Filament Migrator** appears directly below or beside it as supporting text.
3. The descriptor may be omitted only below 96 px or where the complete name is already supplied by adjacent accessible text.
4. Do not shorten the public name to `SL` or turn the descriptor into a tagline.

## Mark construction

- Two open horizontal arcs define the spool. They never close into an infinity or recycling symbol.
- The central rounded-square tile contains three ledger slots. The third slot is deliberately shorter.
- The orange tail always exits to the right and occupies less visual weight than the core.
- The green registration notch is a small rounded square, never a large badge or glow.
- At 16–20 px, omit the orange tail and descriptor; retain the arcs, tile, and green notch.
- Minimum standalone mark: 16 px. Minimum complete horizontal lockup: 180 px.

## Typography

- Product name: `Segoe UI Variable Display`, `Segoe UI`, system sans-serif; compact, bold, slightly tightened.
- Product descriptor: `Cascadia Mono`, `SFMono-Regular`, `Consolas`, monospace; uppercase with restrained tracking.
- Interface copy: `Segoe UI Variable Text`, `Segoe UI`, system sans-serif.
- Never color individual letters in either name.

## Application usage

- Title bar: mark at 34 px, Spool Ledger at 15 px, descriptor at 9 px.
- Window and installer icons: symbol only, with the descriptor supplied by accessible or installer text.
- Loading: the spool arcs rotate around a fixed profile tile; the determinate bar and loaded-of-total count remain authoritative.
- Workflow: profile tiles replace generic circular step markers. Orange marks source provenance; green marks selected or verified destinations.
- Dark theme: deep green-black layers, graphite controls, pale arcs, green registration notches, and small orange source tails.

## Avoid

- Recycling arrows, infinity loops, database cylinders, or cloud-sync symbolism.
- Photorealistic spools, bamboo plants, printer silhouettes, gradients, glows, or 3D effects.
- Reversing the filament tail or adding multiple tails.
- Using orange on destinations, primary actions, or verification states.
- Implying affiliation with Bambu Lab or OrcaSlicer.

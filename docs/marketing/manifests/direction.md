# Spool Ledger campaign direction

## Directions considered

### 1. The Reviewed Route

**Thesis:** A trusted setting moves through a visible, operator-approved route from Orca source to Bambu destination.

**Dials:** Measured energy, medium density, dark workstation ground, shallow depth, locked camera with deliberate route moves, neutral Segoe type, restrained grain, Bambu green plus Orca orange provenance, highly product-literal, quiet electronic score, calm narration.

**Palette:** `bg` and `ink` establish the workstation. `rare` marks source provenance. `brand` marks the reviewed destination and CTA.

**Signature move:** An orange route enters from the source, pauses at the plan ledger, and resolves green only after review. It appears in the logo, the plan transition, and the end lockup.

**Risk:** At weak execution it becomes a generic line animation. The route must always connect to real product stages.

**Wrong for:** A consumer app where review and evidence are not central.

### 2. Profile Avalanche

**Thesis:** A wall of scattered profile cards collapses into one clean destination library.

**Dials:** High energy, high density, deep layered field, continuous camera, bold display type, visible particles, broad color, abstracted UI, score-led, sparse voice.

**Kill result:** Rejected. It decorates volume rather than proving safe review, and a file organizer competitor could use it unchanged.

### 3. Forensic Receipt

**Thesis:** Each migration claim appears as a stamped receipt with its evidence level.

**Dials:** Low energy, sparse density, paper-white ground, flat depth, hard cuts, mono-led type, tactile paper texture, one green signal, reconstructed evidence UI, sound-design-led, title cards only.

**Kill result:** Rejected. It overweights evidence after the run and underexplains the main value of moving trusted settings.

## Chosen direction: The Reviewed Route

The film opens on the argument ground with an orange source route and ends on the product-evidence ground with a green reviewed destination. The route is the signature move: source provenance enters orange, pauses at the frozen plan, then resolves green after operator review. It never presents cloud ID assignment or AMS verification as automatic.

### Accent budget

- **Orca orange (`rare`):** source provenance, route entry, source-side labels only.
- **Bambu green (`brand`):** destination, reviewed action, verified local evidence, CTA.
- **Does not apply to:** body copy, neutral UI structure, unverified cloud or AMS states.

### Sound

Narration leads. The score stays low, modern, and mechanical, with soft route pulses and no rumble bed.

### Execution rules

- Use real Spool Ledger screenshots and the approved live Overture recording.
- Hide account IDs and filesystem paths.
- Use only approved profile names in public assets.
- Keep local creation, Bambu loading, cloud ID assignment, and AMS verification separate.
- Use no endorsement language and no customer-facing em dashes.
